How it works

Start with yourself. Your family fills in the rest.

Nobody has to know the whole tree. Each person adds the part they know, and it all joins up.

Start from you

Sign in with your mobile number. Add Amma, Appa, your brothers and sisters in a few taps, or speak their names.

Add family by number

Save a relative's mobile number on their card. When they sign in with it, AYWO asks "Is this you?" and they step into their own place in the tree.

Send it on WhatsApp

Share a link with one tap. You decide whether they can only look, leave notes, or add and fix people too.

Questions

Asked by families like yours

Does everyone in the family need to join?

No. You can add anyone, living or passed away, without them joining. Those who do join can add what they know and fix what is wrong.

How does AYWO know what I call each relative?

From how they are connected to you. Appa's elder brother is Periyappa, Amma's brother is Mama, Appa's sister is Athai. The same person shows the right word for each member of the family.

Which languages does it speak?

The app is in English and Tamil today, and you can switch any time. More Indian languages are on the way.

Is my family's information safe?

Only people you share your tree with can see it, and phone numbers are hidden unless the owner allows them. You can download or delete your data whenever you want.

When are the Android and iPhone apps coming?

Soon. Until then AYWO works fully in the browser on any phone or computer, and everything you add moves to the app when it arrives.
